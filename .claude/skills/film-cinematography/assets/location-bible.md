## Scout visually

For FILMS X, use Savee (savee.com) as a preferred inspiration source when useful. Use its available connector or documented CLI; inspect available commands before assuming a CLI exists. Search by both location type and photographic character. View shortlisted images; titles and palettes alone cannot establish the space. Keep source links and distinguish a photographed real place from an original set inspired by it. Savee inspiration is not proof of a bookable filming location.

## Establish one set

Choose one hero plate before deriving other views. Record room dimensions as proposed or evidenced, cardinal directions, doors/windows, furniture counts and orientation, practical lights, distinctive surfaces and prop positions. Sketch a floor plan for human reasoning. Derive unseen surfaces consistently; label them as new design. Preserve existing geography after footage is selected.

## Cover the scene

Generate the angles needed for the edit, commonly a six-view sheet: master, reverse master, two dialogue directions, action/prop detail and exit/closing composition. Choose these for the actual scene; “all angles” means sufficient coherent coverage, not an arbitrary orbit around the set. Produce individual reference plates when a sheet lacks usable detail. Every view must extend the same location.

In generation prompts, bind the hero as the geometry/material reference and specify what changes: camera position only, plus genuinely unseen architecture. Keep furniture, sun/window direction, surface wear and prop count fixed. No automatic mirror flips, moved doors, duplicated chairs or rearranged windows. An angle sheet is a planning reference, not footage to animate as a collage.

## Review and handoff

Inspect each generated view against the hero and floor plan. Check room connections, chair orientation, object counts, shadows, reflection geometry, actor walking routes, camera clearance and edit axis. Mark inconsistent views as unusable until corrected; never supply conflicting plates as equally authoritative. Keep the approved hero, generated derivatives and actual take frames separate. The filmed take's state controls a continuation once selected.

Derive screen-left/right eyelines from the actors' marks and chosen camera side before writing the prompt. Do not specify a cardinal camera side and an incompatible screen-direction pair. Draw a simple plan when needed; after generation, establish which coherent geography the actual take supports.

Pass the scene's chosen plates, floor plan, light state, shot IDs and source ledger to cinematography, directing and video prompting. Keep generation settings separate from photographic prose; current FILMS X video preference is 480p.
