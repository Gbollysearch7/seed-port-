# Look and coverage worksheet

## Coherent look

- Dramatic intention and viewer relationship to the scene.
- Aspect ratio and delivery resolution; distinguish requested settings from inspected output.
- Camera rendering / lens family, expressed in visible terms.
- Key source and direction, fill or negative fill, practicals, exposure intention.
- Dominant materials and palette, skin treatment, contrast and texture.
- One photographic contrast that changes with the story, if appropriate.

## Coverage

| Shot | Information and point of view | Position / framing / approximate lens | Depth and focus | Light on this subject | Move or hold, with cue | Join to adjacent shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Useful distinctions

- A wide lens close to an actor and a longer lens farther away give different spatial relationships, even if the face has similar size in the frame.
- A dolly changes viewpoint through space; a zoom changes field of view from one position. Specify the intended effect rather than treating the words as interchangeable.
- Source continuity means preserving the room's source, not always lighting from screen left in every reverse angle.
- A camera-view diagram depicts projected composition. A floor plan depicts room geometry. Provide only the type the recipient can use, with an explicit role.
- Darker does not automatically mean more dramatic. Preserve whatever detail carries the scene's information.
- Generated grain or simulated film stock is not an applied LUT. Actual grading and adjacent-shot matching are post-production tasks.

## Research context

[Higgsfield's Cinema Studio development account](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/how-we-built-cinema-studio) describes testing and exaggerating simulated lens character. Treat that as a vendor account of its product, not proof of physically exact optics in another provider. [Camera-control guide](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-video-camera-control) motivates explicit start/end framing and available controls; its repeatability claims still require testing. Consult these for examples, not immutable model specifications.
