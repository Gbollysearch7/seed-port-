# Film Editing

Direct attention across time and shots. Choose cuts by their dramatic function, then make their picture, sound and continuity requirements explicit. A single held shot can be stronger than an added cut.

## Establish the edit

Read the selected script/takes, user corrections, performance, blocking, voice, sound and visual treatment. Record actual source versions and branch before editing. Preserve approved dialogue, key actions and delivery constraints. Treat an edit-plan request as planning, not permission for new paid generation.

Choose the needed mode: design coverage before production, translate approved cuts into generation instructions, assemble available footage, or diagnose an existing sequence. Do not claim an edit is implemented when only a prompt or plan exists. Use an authorised local media tool or the user's requested editor; distinguish native editor actions from preprocessing performed elsewhere.

## Decide why and when to cut

Identify the outgoing performance beat and the incoming information. Explain why attention changes now: reaction, discovery, action, spatial relationship, time compression, comparison or deliberate contrast. Vary duration according to the event, not a fixed number of cuts per second. Do not cut on every sentence, musical beat or generation boundary by default.

Use [cuts and transitions](references/cuts-and-transitions.md) for the main technique families and [the edit plan](assets/edit-plan.md) for a multi-shot sequence. The vocabulary is overlapping, not exhaustive: a reaction edit can be a hard picture cut with an audio L-cut. Distinguish shot size, camera motion, coverage patterns, edits and transition effects.

For each significant join establish:

- Outgoing state and incoming point of attention, including emotional momentum.
- Camera side, angle, framing, eyeline, direction, action phase and relevant offscreen states.
- Exact picture boundary and separate audio boundaries, with stable speaker identity and sync.
- Available or required source handles, including motion through an action match.
- A fallback that preserves the story when planned coverage is missing or unstable.

A J-cut starts incoming audio before its picture. An L-cut keeps outgoing audio after its picture has changed. Neither requires an audio crossfade. Keep the actual speaker audible and the visible listener silent where appropriate. Do not slip synchronised dialogue arbitrarily to manufacture overlap.

## Prepare cinematography and generated coverage

Make framing show the gesture, contact or reaction required by the cut. Coordinate actor route, camera position, light and focus across views. Maintain coherent geography; an intentional axis change needs a readable spatial plan. Camera moves and lens changes are not substitutes for editorial motivation.

Generation jobs and editorial shots are different units. One job may contain several cuts, or provide a single shot with handles. Prompt the intended transition and verify actual output before choosing final edit points. A requested two-second hold is a project choice, not a universal boundary rule; matched moving action needs continued motion, not a forced stop.

Use actual selected frame and performance states for continuations. If near-matching views jump, compare clean trims, a motivated new angle, useful reaction coverage and an audio bridge. Do not automatically apply dissolves, morphs, freeze frames or interpolation to conceal a seam. Verify any such treatment against faces, hands, sync and the desired visual language.

## Assemble and verify

Keep originals and record source in/out points, timeline positions, audio ranges and transitions. Calculate duration from the finished timeline, including real overlaps, gaps and black; do not assume every transition changes runtime the same way. Preserve the user's resolution and intended cadence.

Review normal-speed playback with audio and joins in context, then inspect suspect frames or waveforms. Confirm the actual exported file as well as the timeline. Use [the take/sequence review](assets/take-review.md); mark unavailable checks not checked. Stills and transcripts do not establish invisible joins, acting or sound continuity.

For revisions, identify the observed problem and the smallest useful repair. Keep successful performances and existing authorisation intact. Do not request repeated approval for routine authorised edits, or infer authorisation for new generation from a quality problem. Return the cut or plan, a concise account of changes, measured delivery details and any material review limits.
