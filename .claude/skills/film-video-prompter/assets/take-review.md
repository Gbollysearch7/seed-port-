Examples of significant defects: unintelligible key dialogue, wrong speaker, unmistakable identity change, impossible contact during an important action, an unexplained continuity jump, or an outburst absent from the delivered performance. Identify these explicitly; do not bury them in a general “looks good.” Judge incidental imperfections at intended viewing size and in context rather than treating every blurred frame as a failure.

## Disposition

- **Usable within reviewed scope:** Relevant checks have passed; record any accepted limitation.
- **Provisional preview:** One or more material checks remain unperformed. State which.
- **Repair candidate:** A specific defect needs editing, replacement coverage or a revised take before a finished-quality claim.

Selected disposition and reason:

If a repair requires new generation, record its purpose and preserve the original; follow the existing authorisation and retry budget. This review does not impose a new permission step on routine authorised local edits and does not authorise automatic paid retries.

After a repair, review the affected passage and its neighbours, plus any checks affected by re-export. Do not claim the entire film was watched when only selected frames or joins were inspected.
