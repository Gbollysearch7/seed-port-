# Prompt assembly

Use as a flexible checklist, not a compulsory long template. Omit irrelevant sections; resolve contradictions before adding detail.

1. Scene intention and output form: single take or edited sequence, genre, approximate pace.
2. Reference bindings and priority: which image identifies each person, outfit, place or prop; which defines composition only.
3. Opening physical state: positions, facing, relevant objects and hands, camera side and initial attention.
4. Ordered shot beats: framing, action, listener response, movement/focus cue and cut where needed.
5. Exact speech and delivery integrated with those beats; stable voice identities separate from scene performance.
6. Light and image character that must persist as the camera changes.
7. Sound events and perspective, including deliberate pauses.
8. Final physical state and edit handoff; only essential exclusions.

## Original compact example

Model-neutral, unrendered example for a family drama:

Mother face and mother wardrobe references depict one woman; boy face and boy wardrobe depict one child. Kitchen reference defines the existing room, furniture and window. Start with the boy seated at screen right. Mother enters the shot from the near-left room entrance carrying two plates, one in each hand. A short lateral track follows her to the table and settles before she speaks. Use a natural-width view with the boy's shoulder briefly in the foreground; keep the route and both plates visible.

She places the larger portion before him, saying, "Here you go," to make the meal feel ordinary. He checks her smaller portion before asking, "That's all you're having?" Let him watch her response rather than glance repeatedly between the plates. Window light models their faces; the room's corners retain detail. End with both plates supported on the table, the mother seated left and the boy right. Exact shot timings and the remaining exchange belong in the production plan before submission.

## Compression check

Can the recipient tell who acts, why the response follows, where everyone begins and ends, what the camera observes and what is spoken? Remove duplicate adjectives and prohibitions before removing the causal action or the listener's task. Split a request if time or complexity is implausible, while preserving the intended scene in the edit.
