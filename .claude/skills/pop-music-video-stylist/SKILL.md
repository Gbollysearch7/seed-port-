# Pop Music Video Stylist

Build a memorable silhouette that belongs to the performer and works across the actual actions in the video. Preserve boldness when requested: fitted tops, high-shine surfaces, leather, denim, flares, jewellery and glamour are design choices, not defects to simplify away.

## Read the fashion brief precisely

Recover selected identities, body proportions, current look IDs, story day, activity, location, lighting and the user's material/fit choices. Distinguish tight fit from exposed skin, a flare from a wide straight leg, coated denim from leather, and metallic jersey from rigid metal. Do not substitute an oversized tee or conservative tailoring for a requested fitted performance look.

Treat a celebrity or video reference as an aesthetic starting point. Extract silhouette, finish, proportion, accessory scale and attitude; preserve the project's own faces and cultural setting. For an exact named video outfit, inspect that reference before claiming a match. When the reference is broad, describe the proposed original look rather than claiming a celebrity wore it.

For actual designer items, verify construction and imagery on official product/collection pages, using a credited retailer only when needed. Record what the source proves. Do not invent labels, product codes, availability or endorsements. Design research does not authorize shopping.

## Style a complete moving person

Define top, lower garment, shoes, layers, accessories and grooming coordination. Specify material, opacity, neckline, sleeve, tuck, waist height, closures, leg shape and hem. Preserve skin tone, tattoos, hair and physique from the identity reference. A tight garment must follow the body, not redesign it.

Give each look a visual priority. For a shiny top and leather flares, separate the surfaces through texture, reflected highlight width or value rather than erasing either requested finish. Keep material highlights within useful exposure so the garment remains readable beside the face. Check fine ribs, sequins and chains at the intended output size without imposing a universal ban on detail.

Use the scene's actions as a fit test: sitting in a vehicle, reaching, gifting, embracing, dancing and a football kick stress different seams, hems and shoes. Specify plausible ease or stretch while retaining the requested silhouette. Avoid secretly switching footwear or trousers between contiguous shots. Track any intended changes as new look/state IDs.

Coordinate couples through two or three shared cues such as finish, metal or tonal family. Give both people independent taste and an active role. Do not make the partner an accessory or prescribe one dress silhouette for every woman. Separate an everyday or performance look from a ceremony look when the story actually changes occasion.

## Reference assets and continuity

Use [the look contract](references/look-contract.md) for the reusable garment and state record. Bind identity, body silhouette and wardrobe to the same stable character ID. Keep canonical portraits intact.

In FILMS X, portraits and neck-down wardrobe references are separate. Respect that project preference; do not automatically impose it on unrelated work. Use front, three-quarter and back wardrobe views when they answer construction questions, with footwear visible and no extra pose panels. The user's current panel count overrides this example.

A wardrobe sheet is an appearance reference, not automatically a video's opening frame. In a reference-to-video project, follow [the reference and performance handoff](references/reference-to-video.md). Do not substitute stills-to-video merely because a tool supports it more easily.

A new look is a proposal until selected. Review generated outputs for fit, garment count, closures, opacity, seams, footwear, accessory ownership and consistency across views. Report deviations; do not label an inconsistent sheet locked. Preserve earlier versions. A request to build this skill or write a styling plan does not itself authorize renders, uploads or video jobs; carry out only the generation the user actually requested and do not extend it to unrequested variants or retries.

## Deliver at the right stage

For design, return the complete look, why it suits the action, material sources if used and unresolved choices. For an authorized reference render, save the image and exact prompt beside a look manifest, distinguish generated approximation from an exact designer product, and state review limits. For video handoff, provide ordered reference roles, look state, physical actions and optional audio segment metadata. Do not promise temporal realism or lip sync based on a successful still image.
