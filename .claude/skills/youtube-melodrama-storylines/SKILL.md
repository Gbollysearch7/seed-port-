# YouTube Melodrama Storylines

Build an emotionally legible story with a strong promise, escalating choices and satisfying consequences. Treat the selected successful examples as craft evidence, not a proven formula for views.

Read [engine-patterns.md](references/engine-patterns.md) for development. Read [research-evidence.md](references/research-evidence.md) for source comparison and attribution; it distinguishes full transcript reading from sampled passages.

## Establish the brief

Infer the mode: **Explore** for differentiated options, **Develop** for one complete storyline, **Diagnose** for an existing story, **Research** for new references. Preserve locked project facts. Do not replace an approved story just because a reference uses another device.

Use context for audience, world, runtime, tone and ending. Ask only for missing facts that materially change the work. For an otherwise unspecified request for a movie like the research sample, state provisional assumptions of a contemporary Nigerian setting and roughly 40–45 minutes. Explicit project context overrides these defaults. Short formats need fewer turns, not a compressed feature.

Explore normally yields three distinct premises. Develop yields one resolved treatment. Analysis alone does not authorize a new screenplay or media generation.

## Build the engine

1. **Promise:** Identify who is mistreated, what they pursue, who obstructs them, what they could lose and the reversal the audience anticipates. Translate a sensational title into an actual dramatic question.
2. **Mechanism:** Choose one primary engine from the reference. Add a secondary engine only if it causes or complicates the main conflict. Do not stack unrelated family, pregnancy, royal and ownership reveals to extend runtime.
3. **Agency:** Give the lead a practical goal, capability, vulnerability, imperfect tactic and boundary. Show a specific act of skill, kindness or courage before its reward. Enduring abuse alone is not agency; a protector cannot make every consequential choice.
4. **Opposition:** Define what the antagonist controls—employment, housing, reputation, family access, land or approval—and why others comply. Give them a plausible self-justification and changing tactics. Escalations must change options or consequences, not merely repeat insults.
5. **Knowledge:** Record when the audience and each major character learn important truths. Early audience knowledge creates anticipation; concealed facts create curiosity. A title that advertises the hidden identity already changes the viewing experience. A flash-forward must catch up with new meaning and leave room for consequences.
6. **Causality:** Plant the skill, document, witness, rule or relationship enabling the reversal. Specify access, credibility, what the proof establishes, why it was unavailable and how it can be contested. Explain delayed intervention rather than allowing harm merely to pad the runtime.
7. **Cost:** Make a central choice risk something valued. Romantic care should become credible before status offers benefits. Secrecy and secret love tests require accountability, anger and repair; discovering wealth does not restore trust automatically.
8. **Payoff:** Match the repair to the harm: restored wages, protected livelihood, truthful records, family boundaries, mutual commitment or a changed institution. Separate forgiveness from renewed access. Wealth cannot establish that a person deserved dignity. Resolve the advertised main question even when a sequel remains possible.

## Develop the treatment

Build the causal spine: disruption → consequential response → counteraction → narrowing options → costly choice → earned reversal → aftermath. This is a diagnostic sequence, not a fixed number of acts or mandatory reveal percentage.

Open near meaningful friction: a wound, impending loss, difficult choice or partial reversal. Include warmth and humour that change trust or prepare a later loss. Dialogue should express character tactics and personality; avoid making every exchange the same question-and-answer banter.

For each beat record:

`ID | approximate time range | observable event | character choice | resulting change | audience/character knowledge | setup/payoff`

Allocate time for comprehension, silence and transitions. Flag long stretches without change. Remove redundant lectures, insults, emotional restatements and musical padding. Keep dialogue examples brief unless spoken scenes are requested. Hand approved story packets to film-script-writer for screenplays and film-dialogue-writer for dialogue passes.

## Deliver and review

A development packet includes:

- Brief interpretation and assumptions; emotional promise, controlling idea and logline.
- Primary engine, character drives and locked world facts.
- Complete treatment including ending; timed causal beat sheet.
- Knowledge/reveal ledger and setup/payoff ledger, combined when concise.
- Ending test: which opening harm/question is answered, what it cost and what changes afterward.
- Material weaknesses or open decisions; handoff identifying required beat outcomes and permitted screenplay choices.

When packaging is requested, offer a few truthful title angles tied to actual events. Do not invent thumbnail scenes, render images or promise view counts.

Repair these failures before delivery:

- Major turns lack an earlier action, planted fact or credible discovery.
- The lead has no consequential choices before rescue or a status reveal.
- The payoff implies dignity would be undeserved if the victim remained poor.
- Escalations repeat rather than change the cost, tactic or options.
- Evidence, deadlines, travel, assets, ownership or family facts contradict each other.
- The ending fails the title promise or stops before showing consequences.
- The story reproduces a reference's distinctive combination of events. Changing names is insufficient: change the setting, occupation, relationship conflict, reversal mechanism and climax in substance.

Respect current workspace media preferences. Story development does not authorize image, voice or video generation. Do not identify a production model from visual appearance.

## Research workflow

Freeze the requested recommendation set instead of recursively following sidebars. Deduplicate, apply the user's view threshold and prioritize their requested order. Record URL, observation date, view count, upload date, duration and actual reading coverage. Inspect transcripts and descriptions; distinguish dialogue from visual events a transcript cannot establish. Report missing or partial coverage plainly.

Separate observed craft, plausible audience appeal and unavailable performance evidence. Personalized recommendations and successful examples omit failures. Public views cannot establish retention, click-through rate, traffic sources, revenue or which plot device caused success. Attribute model/tool claims only when credited. Extract abstract mechanics and create original material rather than reproducing scripts.
