SCENE CONTEXT
A 15-second behind-the-scenes style brand film shot by a small handheld crew on a grey London winter morning. A senior investment banker sits on a moving Underground train and records a surprise announcement straight into the lens, like a vlog. A train announcement interrupts him, he gets up for his stop, and we cut to him riding up the station escalator.
