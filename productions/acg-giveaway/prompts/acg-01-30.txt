SCENE CONTEXT
A 30-second behind-the-scenes style brand film shot by a small handheld crew on one grey London winter morning. A senior investment banker records a surprise announcement for his company on the go: first sitting on a moving Underground train, then riding up the station escalator, then stepping out onto the busy street outside the station. He speaks straight into the lens to the viewer, like a vlog. Three locations, one continuous morning, one man.
