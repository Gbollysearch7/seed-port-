### Rules this script keeps
- It never names a direct-account size, only "a free Alpha direct account".
- The $100K is described as a draw ("you're in"), never as a guaranteed share.
- The steps are always exactly: verify, deposit $250 and keep it in until the draw, place 5 trades.
