# FOLLOW THE BAG · first 15 seconds (final edit time)

One continuous camera that never cuts; it follows the bag. Hidden wipe at 0:10 (backpack swings across the lens).
Speed ramp: slow on the burger, the seal and the hand-off; fast through the walking.

---

**INT. BOLLY EATS KITCHEN · BLUE HOUR**

*Warm amber heat lamps over a steel pass. Through the glass front door at the back, the wet neon street glows blue.*

**0:00–0:03 · THE BURGER**
Macro on the flat-top. A smash patty sizzles, cheese melting. The CHEF's black-gloved hand slides the steel spatula under it and lifts it onto a toasted bun waiting on the pass. *(slow)*

**0:03–0:05 · INTO THE BOX**
He crowns it, then sets the burger into an open turmeric Bolly Eats burger box on the pass and snaps the lid shut. A carton of fries sits beside it. The camera drifts back, still on the food.

**0:05–0:07 · THE SEAL**
Box and fries go into the turmeric-and-maroon Bolly Eats paper bag, and the shake goes into the maroon carrier. He folds the top of the bag over and presses the round steam-mark sticker down with his thumb. *(slow, ramped right down on the press)*

**0:07–0:09 · THE HAND-OFF**
He slides the bag across the pass. The RIDER's fingerless-gloved hand takes it. *(slow)* The camera follows the bag as the rider turns, unzips the turmeric backpack sitting on the counter, lowers the bag into the foil-lined inside and zips it shut.

**0:09–0:10 · WIPE**
He swings the backpack onto his shoulders, and the turmeric side with the big "bolly eats." fills the whole lens.

---

**EXT. HIGH STREET · BLUE HOUR, JUST AFTER RAIN**

**0:10–0:12 · OUT THE DOOR**
Out of the turmeric, behind the rider: he pushes through the glass door onto the wet pavement. Pink and amber neon reflects in the puddles. His e-bike waits at the kerb.

**0:12–0:15 · AWAY**
He clips his turmeric helmet, swings onto the bike and pushes off. The camera rises and chases the turmeric backpack down the shining street. *(ramps fast)* Continues into Segment 2 (the bus wipe).

---

**Where the burger goes:** grill → bun on the steel pass → turmeric burger box (lid snapped) → paper bag (sealed) → rider's insulated backpack. That chain is the "bag" the camera follows for the whole film.

**Sound:** sizzle, lid snap, paper fold, sticker press, zip, door, rain hiss, bike whir. Music goes in during the edit.
